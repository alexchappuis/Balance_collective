What We Believe.

Real wellness isn't complicated. We've just been pulled toward extremes and quick fixes, and away from what's simple and sustainable. Balance Collective creates wellness experiences for teams built around what actually matters - movement, nourishment, rest, and connection - in a way that fits real, busy lives.

About

Hi, I'm Isa.

I'm an NBHWC board-certified health and wellness coach and the founder of Balance Collective. I studied psychology at Northeastern University and grew up in a Puerto Rican household where food, family, and taking care of each other were central to everything. Those roots have shaped everything I believe about wellness.

At one point I drifted from those roots, my energy dropped, my mind felt overwhelmed, and I found myself doing movement I didn't enjoy with a complicated relationship to my health. Returning to the simple habits I grew up with changed everything.

I created Balance Collective because I believe taking care of yourself should feel simple, sustainable, and actually good.

— Isa, Founder of Balance Collective

Community

Club Balance.

Real moments, real connection, real wellness. Club Balance is an in-person community in Houston where people come together, feel safe, and actually enjoy taking care of themselves — whether that means making a new friend, getting creative, moving in a way that feels good, or simply being in a room with other people.

Every Club Balance event is designed around a different pillar of health — movement, nutrition, mental health, creativity, sleep, community, purpose, and nature — because wellness is so much more than just working out and eating well.
